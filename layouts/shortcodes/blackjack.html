A quick guide

How to play

Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over.

  1. Deal a hand. You and the dealer each get two cards. One of the dealer’s cards stays face down.
  2. Hit to take another card. You can hit again as long as your total is below 21.
  3. Stand when you want to keep your total. At 21, you stand automatically.
  4. The dealer reveals their hand and draws until reaching 17 or more. The higher total wins.

A few table rules

  • Bust: go over 21 and you lose immediately. If the dealer busts, you win.
  • Blackjack: an ace plus a 10-point card in your first two cards. It beats any ordinary 21. Two blackjacks tie.
  • Push: equal totals are a tie.
  • The dealer stands on all 17s, including “soft 17” (an ace counted as 11).

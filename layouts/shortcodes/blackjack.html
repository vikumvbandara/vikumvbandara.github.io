A quick guide

How to play

Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over.

  1. Choose a wager, then deal. Start with $1,000 in practice dollars. Set a wager in $5 steps, up to your available balance. You and the dealer each get two cards, with one dealer card face down.
  2. Hit to take another card. You can hit again as long as your total is below 21.
  3. Stand when you want to keep your total. At 21, you stand automatically.
  4. The dealer reveals their hand and draws until reaching 17 or more. The higher total wins.

Dollar scoring

  • Win: earn your wager as profit (1:1). A $25 wager wins $25.
  • Blackjack: earn 1½ times your wager (3:2). A $25 wager wins $37.50.
  • Lose: lose the amount you wagered.
  • Tie: your wager is returned, with $0 won or lost.

Your wager is deducted when cards are dealt. A win returns that wager plus your profit. “Net won / lost” totals completed hands. If you have less than $5, reset to $1,000 to keep playing. Resetting also clears your results.

A few table rules

  • Bust: go over 21 and you lose immediately. If the dealer busts, you win.
  • Blackjack: an ace plus a 10-point card in your first two cards. It beats any ordinary 21. Two blackjacks tie.
  • Push: equal totals are a tie.
  • The dealer stands on all 17s, including “soft 17” (an ace counted as 11).

This practice table uses a freshly shuffled 52-card deck for every hand. No splitting, doubling, or insurance. Practice dollars have no cash value. Your balance and results reset when you reload the page.

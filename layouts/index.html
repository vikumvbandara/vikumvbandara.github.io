Selected research

Bulgarian solitaire with distinguishable cells

With Dr. Svetlana Poznanović, I study the movement of individual labeled cells in Bulgarian solitaire, connecting Ferrers diagrams, permutation cycles, and periodic orbits.

Read the preprint (PDF)

Collatz conjecture–driven cryptographic framework

With Dr. Rajitha Ranasinghe, I investigated constructions inspired by Collatz sequences and implemented proposed encryption and decryption algorithms in Python. This paper grew out of my undergraduate thesis at the University of Peradeniya.

Journal of Discrete Mathematical Sciences & Cryptography · 2026

Read the paper (PDF)
Read my undergraduate thesis (PDF)

Symmetric encryption using snake graphs and supermagic covering

With Dr. Rajitha Ranasinghe, I studied graph-labeling constructions for symmetric encryption using snake graphs and supermagic coverings.

Journal of the National Science Foundation of Sri Lanka · 2024

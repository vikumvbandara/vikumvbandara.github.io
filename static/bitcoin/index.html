ⓘHow to read this dashboard+

Evidence you can inspect

Four rule-based agents evaluate trend, momentum, participation, and risk using completed market candles. Every result follows fixed, inspectable thresholds. No AI model or news research is used in this mode.

Signal labels

Buy setup requires a weighted rule score of at least 60, bullish trend and momentum agents, and passing risk and data checks. Watch means partial support. Wait means no qualifying setup or a blocking concern. Unavailable means the market data cannot support analysis.

A starting point for research

The score measures rule alignment, not the chance of a profitable trade. Agents share price and volume data, so their results can be correlated. Both strategies are unvalidated spot-buy research, with no trade execution. Test with historical and paper trading results before relying on signals.

Freshness & alerts

Analysis uses completed daily candles, not the current live price. Local rules are recalculated as the view refreshes. Optional daily monitoring runs on this computer and sends eligible buy signals to Telegram. Demo results never trigger alerts.
